# Camper

Everything about our camper and its smart-home setup: the Home Assistant config, the ESP32 firmware, the Jetson voice and AI helpers, the truck OBD-II link, build guides, and notes on what was done and why.

**The camper:** 2018 Forest River Grey Wolf 26DJSE travel trailer. Sticker weights: 4,521 lb dry, 3,015 lb cargo. It's towed by a 2016 Toyota Sequoia. Our dog Daisy lives in the camper too.

> Passwords, tokens, Wi-Fi details, the full VIN and the home address are **not** in this repo. Files that need them read from `secrets.yaml`. See the `*.example` files.

## What runs where

| Device | Address | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Raspberry Pi 4, Home Assistant OS | 192.168.0.168:8123 (remote: Tailscale) | The main hub: dashboards, automations, Zigbee (HUSBZB-1 stick) |
| Jetson Orin | 192.168.0.173 | "Camper Brain" local AI chat (:8090), "Hey Jarvis" voice satellite, sound watcher (smoke alarm and barking), camera gestures, music |
| ODROID | 192.168.0.169 | Desktop where this repo is kept and edited |
| ESP32 bench rig | 192.168.0.73 | Awning and wall-panel firmware, **still on the bench, not wired into the camper yet** |
| ELM327 Bluetooth adapter → Torque Pro | — | Sequoia live data and trouble codes into Home Assistant |

## What it does

- **Climate:** Micro-Air EasyTouch thermostat over Bluetooth (patched integration), smart heat/cool setpoints, a work-night schedule, a running glow on the dashboard. Aqara Zigbee temperature sensors inside and under the camper, plus an underbelly heater on a smart plug.
- **Dehumidifier:** Midea unit through `midea_ac_lan`, with a tank-full alert and its own page.
- **Safety:** smoke-alarm and dog-bark detection on the Jetson, Daisy heat alerts, battery and weather reminders before work, a 1 PM summary, a safe-drive check and find-my-phone.
- **Stereo:** Furrion Bluetooth stereo (works only with HFP turned off). Music is ad-free only: Spotify, YouTube Premium or listener-supported stations.
- **Weather and fishing:** met.no, NWS and Open-Meteo weather page; solunar and pressure-trend Fishing page.
- **Shopping list:** quick-add picture buttons and a reminder when you arrive at Walmart.
- **Truck:** Sequoia gauges page and a Drive page (`/drive`) for the dash tablet.
- **Lights:** WLED controllers: a GLEDOPTO for the awning strip, still offline until its enclosure is built, and an Athom unit for the RGB strip.
- **Inverter:** Traveller 3000 W, not installed yet. The plan is in `docs/guides/Inverter-Install-Guide.pdf`.

## Dashboard rules

- Big pictures and big text. Use pictures, not text lists, for low vision.
- One glow color per device on every page. Peach sections, white inner cards.
- After **every** edit to `dashboards/camper.yaml`, run `python3 make_phone_dashboard.py` to rebuild the tablet dashboard.
- Before editing a file on the Pi, copy it to `/config/claude_backups/` with a `.bak-preclaude-<what>-<date>` name.
- Never change real thermostat setpoints or modes just to test something.

## Restoring onto a fresh Home Assistant

1. Copy `home-assistant/*` into `/config`.
2. Create `/config/secrets.yaml` from the example file.
3. Install from HACS: `midea_ac_lan`, `alarmo`, `aarlo`, `feedparser`, plus the Lovelace cards the dashboard uses (Mushroom, card-mod and others).
4. Restart, then pair the Zigbee sensors and set up the Bluetooth thermostat again. The steps are in `docs/notes/`.
