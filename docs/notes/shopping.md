# project-camper-shopping

> Camper shopping list setup in HA - quick-add buttons, Walmart Shelby arrival reminder, Amazon-only items

Shopping list = `todo.shopping_list` (Shopping page, /lovelace-camper/shopping). Built 2026-10-17:
- `sensor.shopping_list_items` (trigger-based template, attribute `items`) gives the item names.
- `script.camper_shopping_toggle(item)`: adds an item, or removes it if it's already open; brings back a checked-off duplicate.
- The Quick Add grid has 12 items; green means it's on the list. "Tank treatment (Amazon)" is the user's: they buy tank treatment on Amazon.
- Stores: **Walmart Supercenter, Shelby NC** (`zone.walmart_shelby`, YAML zone, 200 m) plus, at the user's request, all local Walmarts within ~35 mi of Jonesville: Union, Gaffney, Spartanburg x2 + Cedar Springs Neighborhood Market, Boiling Springs SC, Kings Mountain NM. Each is a zone named "Walmart <Town> - <street>". Forest City and Cherryville were left out as too far. They also use Walmart delivery. The automation condition is startswith('Walmart') or startswith('Dollar General').
- They also shop at Dollar General a lot. **17 DGs** in Gaffney (8: Chesnee Hwy added from DG listings; "Corry St"/"Macason St" renamed Old Georgia Hwy/E Frederick St; Durham Alley + Bonner Rd removed at user request, not in DG listings), Shelby (8) and Pacolet (1) are YAML zones named "Dollar General <Town> - <street>" (radius 120, from OSM/Nominatim). The user said "add all of them".
- They also shop at **Ingles**: 16 Ingles zones within ~35 mi (radius 150). Condition: startswith(('Walmart','Dollar General','Ingles')).
- `automation.shopping_list_when_arriving_at_walmart` (alias "…at a Store"): a person state change (`to: ~`) held for 2 min, condition the state is "Walmart Shelby" or starts with "Dollar General". The title says Walmart or Dollar General.
- Original note on that automation: after 2 min in the zone it sends the list to whoever arrived. Items with "amazon" in the name are listed as "Order on Amazon: …". Nothing is sent if the list is empty.
- The Quick Add grid has 18 items (Cereal + "Little Debbie cakes" added for Nadine, who loves them. The Cereal button opens more-info for `input_select.shopping_cereal` (28 brands + "Pick a cereal..."); `automation.shopping_cereal_picked` adds "Cereal - <name>" once and resets the dropdown. laundry detergent, "Dawn dish soap" (they use Dawn), paper plates, and "Coffee creamer (caramel macchiato)" (beverage box pic, after Coffee pods) were added later). Each has `picture: /local/shopping/<slug>.png`, using Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D, MIT, in www/shopping/. Toilet paper uses the toilet emoji, paper towels the roll, and tank treatment the lotion bottle.
