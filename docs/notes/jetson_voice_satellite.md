> "Hey Jarvis" voice satellite on the Jetson (Brio webcam mic) whose spoken replies go nowhere; Bluetooth speaker is on the Pi


Found 2026-10-21 (built 2026-10-08, no notes were written then). Jetson user systemd units (`systemctl --user`, ~/.config/systemd/user, files in ~/camper_voice): voice-satellite (wyoming_satellite "Camper Jetson", :10700), voice-wakeword (openWakeWord, 127.0.0.1:10400, wake word **hey_jarvis**), voice-whisper (faster-whisper base-int8 CPU, :10300 = HA `stt.faster_whisper`), voice-piper (:10200 = HA `tts.piper`). No HA automation forwards satellite replies to `media_player.camper_stereo`. Only one test recording exists (2026-10-08).
