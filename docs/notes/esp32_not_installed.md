# project-camper-esp32-not-installed

> Camper ESP32s not in the camper; awning firmware on the bench rig at .73 since 2026-10-26 (no wall-panel ESP online); readings are test values, safe to test

As of 2026-10-04 neither camper ESP32 is wired into the camper. The "Camper Wall Panel" ESP32 (tanks, battery voltage/current/SoC, water heater gas relay, water pump relay) is powered and online in HA but not connected to anything. The awning ESP32 (`camper-awning-weather`) isn't built yet. `cover.awning` is a template placeholder.

The only real camper system HA controls is the Micro-Air EasyTouch thermostat (heat and A/C). See [[project-camper-easytouch]]. The TP-Link plugs (coffee pot, ceiling fan, power strip) are real network devices.

**Why:** the wall panel's readings are floating ADC noise, not faults. For example, 6.45 V "battery" and 100% SoC aren't real. The user said water heater, gas and awning automations can be tested freely.
**How to apply:** don't report tank, battery or awning values as real problems. It's fine to run water heater, water pump and awning scripts/automations as tests. Still treat the thermostat and TP-Link plugs as live hardware.
