# project-camper-sky-backgrounds

> Live "sky" card backgrounds copied from the Animated Weather Card; on the Weather + Fishing pages since 2026-11-12; the user wants to try them more widely


On 2026-11-12 the user said "im realy loving the anamited cards backgroung colors and the way they change i want to do this to all the cards i think can we try a few".

**How it works:** the card_mod jinja at the top of a style block picks the condition (a map from the weather.forecast_home state to the card's sky class) and the phase (sun.sun elevation < -6 is night, ≥ 6 is day, otherwise dawn if `rising` else dusk, which is the card's own `Di()` rule). It then indexes a palette dict of top,mid,bottom hex colors. The palette was read live from the card's generated CSS (`.animated-weather-card.<cond>[data-phase=..] { --awc-sky-top/mid/bottom }`): 15 conditions × 4 phases. 11 conditions are copied, and light-rain/stormy variants are mapped to rainy/stormy. The background is `linear-gradient(180deg, top 0%, mid 55%, bottom 100%)` plus the card's soft radial glow and bottom scrim.

**Where (first try):** the Weather page `&wx_dark` (Next 24 Hours, This Week, Three Forecasts), plus the Fishing page header, rating card (rating tint over the sky) and its 4 stacks. Script: skybg.py replaced the static slate gradient. Backup claude_backups/dashboards__camper.yaml.bak-preclaude-skybg-20261112-064748.

**How to apply:** to extend to other pages, swap the peach section gradient for the same jinja and background, and make the text white and the tiles glass (see the dark restyle in [[project-camper-weather-page]]). Ask the user page by page. It conflicts with the peach and per-device glow rules in [[project-camper-dashboard-colors]].
